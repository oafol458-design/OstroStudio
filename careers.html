The role

We're expanding our sales team and looking for 10 motivated remote Sales Representatives who are confident on the phone and driven by results.

As a Sales Representative, you'll connect with leads provided by Ostro Studio, introduce our website services, understand each prospect's needs, and work toward closing the sale.

Responsibilities include

  • Contacting qualified leads provided by Ostro Studio
  • Speaking directly with business owners over the phone
  • Presenting our website services clearly and confidently
  • Closing website sales and guiding prospects through the purchasing process
  • Scheduling follow-up calls with interested prospects who need additional time or information
  • Maintaining consistent communication with prospects throughout the sales process

This is primarily a phone-based sales position. Video calls are not required.

This is a full-time role, about 8 hours a day. You set your own schedule. Most of our clients are in North America, so most calls happen during their business hours.

Compensation

This is a 100% commission-based position, with commissions paid twice per week through Stripe or Wise.

20%

30%

Sales Accelerator: reach $10,000 in website sales in a calendar month and your rate rises to 30% for the rest of that month

  • Recurring commissions continue for as long as eligible clients remain subscribed to their care plans
  • There is no fixed salary or hourly wage

Example

Generate $10,000 in website sales and earn at least $2,000 in initial commissions, with every sale after that earning 30%, plus recurring monthly commissions from eligible care plans.

Requirements

  • Excellent spoken and written English
  • Confident and professional communication over the phone
  • Comfortable speaking directly with business owners
  • Self-motivated, reliable, and capable of working independently in a remote environment
  • Access to a phone or computer, stable internet connection, and a quiet environment for calls
  • Strong follow-up and communication skills
  • Previous sales, cold-calling, appointment-setting, or customer-facing experience is an asset but not required
  • A results-driven attitude and willingness to learn
